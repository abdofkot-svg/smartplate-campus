Smarter meal planning. Less avoidable food waste.

Working prototype · Sample data

How it works

SmartPlate estimates daily meal demand from recent sales, suggests a preparation quantity with a small safety buffer, and tracks the difference between meals prepared and meals sold.

This demonstration uses sample data. It is not yet connected to a real cafeteria or a trained AI model.

1. Demand Predictor

Enter meal sales from the last three days.

Enter the recent sales and select Predict demand.

2. Waste Tracker

Compare how many meals were prepared with how many were sold.

Enter the prepared and sold meals to calculate the surplus.

Our future vision

With access to real cafeteria sales and information about holidays or campus events, SmartPlate could make better forecasts. Cafeterias could also explore safe, approved ways to donate suitable surplus food.
